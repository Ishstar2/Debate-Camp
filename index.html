Why we're here

Knowing how to make your case, and how to really listen to someone else's, is one of the most useful things a kid can learn. We didn't think something that important should cost money, so Vortex is free and open to any middle schooler who wants to give it a shot.

How it works

Easy to join. Safe by design.

Every student gets their own coach for a private weekly hour online. A parent registers. No student accounts, no hassle.

1

A parent registers

A two-minute form: your name and email, your child's first name and grade. That's all we collect.

2

Get matched with a coach

We match your child with a coach, find a weekly time that works for your family, and send you a secure link.

3

Meet 1-on-1, weekly

An hour each week, just your child and their coach. You're always welcome to sit in or watch from the next room.

4

Practice & grow

Drills, mock debates, and honest feedback. Your child gets a little better every week, and has fun doing it.

For parents

Your child's safety comes first.

  • Parents are always welcome.Because sessions are one-on-one, we encourage a parent or guardian to be present or nearby. The virtual door is always open.
  • Recorded for transparency.Sessions can be recorded at your request and shared with you, so you always know exactly how your child's time is spent.
  • Private, secure video.Business-grade meetings with waiting rooms and passwords, not open or gaming chat apps. We collect almost nothing and never sell data.
  • COPPA-compliant & parent-led.Registration and all communication go through you, the parent, in line with children's-privacy law.

Questions

Good to know

Who will coach my child? +

Your child's coach is a high-school freshman who is an experienced, passionate debater and volunteers their time. That's exactly why the program is free. A responsible adult supervises the program and handles all safety, privacy, and legal matters. We want this clear up front so there are no surprises once sessions begin.

Is it really free? +

Yes, completely. Vortex Speech & Debate is volunteer-run and there's no cost to families, now or ever.

What ages is it for? +

Students in grades 5–8 (roughly ages 10–14). Because coaching is one-on-one, every session is tailored to your child's exact age and level.

What does a session look like? +

One student, one coach, one hour, every week, online. The coach tailors each session to your child, with a parent always welcome to be present.

Does my child need experience? +

Not at all. Most of our students are brand new to debate. We start with the fundamentals.

What do we need to take part? +

A computer or tablet with a camera and microphone, and a quiet spot to join. That's it.

How do you keep kids safe online? +

Sessions are one-on-one, and we encourage a parent or guardian to be present or nearby. We use secure video with waiting rooms and passwords, can record sessions on request, keep all communication through parents, and a responsible adult oversees the program. See the "For parents" section for details.
